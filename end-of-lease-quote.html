What's included

Every end of lease clean covers the full checklist

No surprise gaps at inspection. This is what's covered as standard.

Kitchen

  • All cupboards washed inside and out
  • Bench tops cleaned
  • Oven (including trays and racks) and cooktop cleaned
  • Exhaust fan and light fittings cleaned

Bathroom

  • All tiles cleaned, grout and ceiling mould free
  • Shower screen cleaned
  • Toilets washed inside and out
  • Vanity, exhaust fan and light fittings cleaned

Floor coverings

  • All floors cleaned and stains removed
  • Tiles and timber floors mopped
  • Carpets cleaned

Windows & coverings

  • Windows cleaned inside, and outside where able
  • Window and sliding door sills and tracks cleaned
  • Venetian blinds cleaned
  • Curtains cleaned (not washed without prior approval)

Bedrooms & laundry

  • Built-in wardrobes cleaned, including doors and mirrors
  • Laundry tub cleaned
  • Laundry walls and floors cleaned

General & keys

  • External areas returned to their start-of-lease condition
  • Gardens weeded, lawns mowed, garage emptied and cleaned
  • Bins emptied
  • Keys, remotes and instruction guides returned

Common questions

Before you book

How much does an end of lease clean cost?

From $500, quoted for your property, excluding optional extras like carpet steam cleaning (from $199) or a deep oven clean. You'll have your price before you book.

What's included?

Every clean follows the full end of lease checklist above: kitchen, bathroom, floors, windows and window coverings, bedrooms, laundry, general areas and keys.

Can you work around my moving date?

Yes. After-hours, evening and weekend cleans are available, and we plan the clean around your handover date. Let us know your key return date when you ask for a quote.

Do you clean carpets too?

Yes. Carpet steam cleaning is available from $199, added on to your end of lease clean.

Which suburbs do you service?

Every suburb from Glenbrook to Blackheath: Glenbrook, Blaxland, Warrimoo, Valley Heights, Springwood, Faulconbridge, Linden, Woodford, Hazelbrook, Lawson, Bullaburra, Wentworth Falls, Leura, Katoomba, Medlow Bath and Blackheath.

Is Blooms a franchise?

No. Blooms is locally owned and run by Allison Land, who lives in the Blue Mountains and is on-site at every clean.
